FINISH GOODS BULK DELETE
========================
Access: Super Admin only.
Location: Finish Goods Aging -> Bulk Delete.

The operation deletes stock_batches for the selected Division(s).
stock_movements child records are deleted first for compatibility with older FK definitions.
The operation DOES NOT delete divisions, users, grading criteria, materials, or Market Return data.
Each affected division is recorded in Activity Logs with action BATCH_BULK_DELETE.
