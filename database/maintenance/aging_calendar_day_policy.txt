AGING / EXPIRY REMAINING CALENDAR-DAY POLICY
============================================
Aging Days = elapsed days from Manufacturing Date to the as-of date.
Expiry Remaining Days = live calendar days from the as-of date through Expiry Date, inclusive.
On the actual Expiry Date the batch is Expired, so Expiry Remaining = 0.

Example as-of 04/Oct/2026:
Manufacturing Date: 01/Oct/2026
Expiry Date:        31/Oct/2026
Aging Days:         3
Expiry Remaining:   28
Total calendar shelf-life: 31 days

Grade percentage calculation is intentionally unchanged so existing division grading thresholds do not shift.
